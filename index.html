We test how well our speech recognition understands names. All names are invented. Please read each sentence naturally, the way you would dictate a quick note after a meeting: casual is fine, and if you stumble, just keep going or record again. Takes about 10 minutes.

The recordings stay on your phone until you send them at the end. They are used only for internal research on speech recognition and deleted when the project ends.

Tip: record about half of the sentences in a noisy place (café, open office, street) and mark it below each sentence. That tells us how background noise affects the result.
